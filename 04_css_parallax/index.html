The journey begins beside a quiet lake. The mountains reflect in the water, and for a moment, everything feels still. I decide to follow the path beyond the shore.

At the Waterfall

The path leads to a waterfall hidden between the mountains. The quiet lake and the rushing water are different parts of the same journey. I stop here for a while before heading home.
